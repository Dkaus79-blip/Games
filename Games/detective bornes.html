How to Play

Goal: Win a best‑of‑5 match by solving 3 cases (rounds) before your opponent.

Match Rules

  • Each match is best‑of‑5.
  • First detective to 3 round wins wins the match.
  • A round ends when a detective reaches 100% Case Solved and is not sabotaged.
  • After each round, everything resets and the next round begins automatically.
  • Winning a match increases your Winning Streak.
  • Losing a match resets your streak to 0.

Card Types

  • 🔍 Investigation cards – increase your case progress. Only one card per turn, and cannot be played while sabotaged.
  • 🚫 Sabotage cards – block or limit your opponent’s progress.
  • 🛠️ Remedy cards – remove sabotage.
  • ⭐ Power cards – permanent abilities or sabotage immunity.

Turn Structure

  1. Draw 1 card at the start of your turn.
  2. Play ONE card.
  3. End turn → draw 1 card.

Cheat Sheet

🔍 Investigation Cards
+10 / +20 / +30 / +40%
• Only 1 card per turn
• Cannot play if sabotaged
🚫 Sabotage Cards
• False Lead – blocks all progress
• Locked Evidence – progress capped at +10% per turn
• Tampered Evidence – lose 10% + blocked
• Witness Vanishes – must fix first
• Camera Offline – lose next turn
🛠️ Remedy Cards
• Case Review – remove all sabotage
• Corrected Evidence – remove one sabotage
• Found Witness – fixes Witness Vanishes
• System Reboot – fixes Camera Offline
⭐ Power Cards
• Sabotage Shield – blocks next sabotage
• Forensics Expert – immune to Tampered Evidence
• Surveillance Access – immune to Camera Offline
• Priority Access – immune to Locked Evidence
• Extra Insight – draw +1 each turn
