📖 Seaside Haven: Sovereign Guide

1. Relaxed Colonization: Days are calm (12 minutes per day). Citizens eat at 08:00 (Breakfast) and 19:00 (Dinner). Granaries protect food reserves from spoilage.

2. Time Controls: Use 1 - 4 or Space / P to adjust simulation speed from pause to 4x speed.

3. Farming & Well Irrigation: Till grass with ⛏️, sow seasonal seeds, and build Wells nearby to speed up growth by +35% and protect against summer heatwaves.

4. Biome Exploration: Unlock all 14 regions. Volcanic calderas yield coal and obsidian; iron crags regenerate iron ore.

5. Naval Supremacy: Build Caravels & War Galleons in the harbor to defeat dungeon bosses and unlock rare relics for your Grand Museum.
