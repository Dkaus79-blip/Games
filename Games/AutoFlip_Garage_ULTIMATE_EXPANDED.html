🛒 1. Flipping & Detailing

Buy low, scrub clean, fix up, and sell high:

  • Junkyard cars come dirty. Click 🚿 Detail & Wash in Workshop to scrub mud off with your mouse for a free +$1,000 value bonus!
  • Repair parts using cash OR using Salvaged Parts from the Pick-a-Part lot.
  • Sell at 🔨 Live Auction for huge profit spikes!

📋 2. Fulfilling Customer Contracts (CRITICAL RULE)

VIP clients pay huge bounties for exact restored rides:

  • Find the car in Classifieds and restore the requested part to 100%.
  • Click Deliver Car & Collect Fee under Contracts.
⚠️ WARNING: Keep the car in your 🔧 Garage Bays! Do NOT deliver it to the Showroom, or the client cannot take delivery!

🏁 3. Drag Strip, Reaction Times & Pink Slips

Launch reaction is everything:

  • Click 🚦 Stage at Tree. The Christmas tree will flash yellow lights, then GREEN.
  • Click 🚀 LAUNCH! exactly when green lights up. Launching during yellow results in a RED LIGHT DQ!
  • Hit 💥 HIT NOS mid-race for an explosive surge.
  • In Pink Slip Mode, the winner takes the opponent's actual car!

🌾 4. Barn Finds & Global Expansion

Pay scouts to find hidden legendary chassis at 0% condition. Rebuild them into $300k+ collector pieces. Once wealthy, expand your empire to Tokyo and Monaco!
