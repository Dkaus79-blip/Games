📖 Vampire Masters: Official Game Guide & Manual

🎓 1. Beginner Tutorial Walkthrough & Quickstart

New to Vampire Masters? Here is your step-by-step road to conquest:

  • Step 1: Citadel Income: Upgrade your Vaults and Crypts to earn passive Gold, Gems, and Blood Essence continuously.
  • Step 2: Dark Portals: Spend Blood Essence and Gems in the Summon Portal to recruit mythical Vampire Lords, Archangels, and Liches.
  • Step 3: Auto-Optimize Squad: Click `🔥 Auto-Optimize ALL` at the top bar to automatically pick your best 4 heroes, slot gear, equip pets, and hire AI Mercenaries!
  • Step 4: Battle & Campaign: Fight through Campaign stages to earn Skill Points and unlock new Bloodline Tree powers!
  • Step 5: Daily Quests: Complete daily tasks for huge gold, gem, and shard payouts!

🎯 2. Target Wishlists & Fake-Out Coffin Reveals

Configure up to 3 targeted wishlist heroes in the Dark Summons Tab. When rolling Mythic or Legendary units, your Wishlist choices gain a 70% Rate-Up Chance!

Watch out for **FAKE-OUT ANIMATIONS** during summons! Standard coffins can crack open with golden light to reveal Mythic or Transcendent Heroes!

🧬 3. Bloodline Mastery & Gear Refinement (+10 Enchanting)

Earn Bloodline Skill Points by completing Campaign stages. Allocate points into Vampiric Might (ATK), Shadow Aegis (HP), or Blood Siphon (Lifesteal).

Visit the Blacksmith tab to enhance weapons and armor up to +10 with Relic Dust to multiply their base stat power!

🌀 4. Abyssal Labyrinth & Real-Time Shadow Expeditions

Navigate the branching Abyssal Labyrinth floor grid to collect powerful battle blessings. Dispatch your extra reserve heroes on Real-Time Expeditions to passively earn medals and celestial marks while you play or sleep!
