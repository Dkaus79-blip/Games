📖 Sanctuary Caretaker's Codex

🐟 1. Economy & Manual Tapping

Tap the big pet cat to generate Fish. Tapping rapidly fills the Meow Frenzy gauge—triggering a 10-second frenzy multiplying all production by 2.5x!

🌌 2. Gacha Banners & Pity System

Spend Fish, Gems, Catnip, and Golden Yarn to summon 100 unique cat species across 6 rarity tiers (Common to Divine). Each banner has guaranteed pity counter mechanics for high-star cats.

  • Duplicate Copies: Every 5 copies automatically promote a cat to a higher Star Rank (up to 5★).
  • Prismatic Awakening: 10 excess copies at 5★ unlock a rainbow Prismatic evolution (+120% Output)!

⚔️ 3. Team Synergies & Tower Raids

Equip up to 5 cats in your active Battle Team. Matching elemental felines activates powerful sanctuary-wide resonance multipliers:

  • 2+ Celestial: +35% Global Production
  • 2+ Mystic: +30% Global Production
  • 2+ Cyber: +25% Global Production
  • 2+ Void: +45% Global Production
  • 2+ Aquatic: +20% Global Production

🔮 4. Dual Prestige Hierarchy

Layer 1 (Astral Rebirth): Trade temporary fish for permanent Golden Yarn to unlock cosmic perks and pull on the Astral Pantheon banner.

Layer 2 (Cosmic Ascension): The ultimate endgame reset. Sacrifice Yarn & fish for Cosmic Cores that grant permanent core multiplicative multipliers.

🧬 5. Lab, Trials & Expeditions

Assign cats to time-based expeditions, roll procedural modifiers in Endless Trials, unlock research trees, and awaken custom traits in the Cat Evolution Lab!
