📖 Pallet Reseller Handbook: Quick Guide

🚦 Start Here: Your First Shift

New games begin with a guided tutorial. You begin with $250. A reliable opening is: buy the $95 Target Clean Customer Returns pallet, unpack it, then inspect and test the items. Put tested items on shelves before opening the doors. Save the $140 Online Marketplace + Shipping upgrade for after your first sale unless online selling is your priority.

🎯 Campaign Goal

Build a sustainable depot by reaching Day 7 with at least $500 cash and 4.8 reputation. The target stays visible in the top bar, and the depot success screen appears after rent is paid.

💻 1. Procurement (Office PC)

Go to your Office PC ➔ LiquiBids to bid on wholesale liquidation pallets (Target, Home Depot, Best Buy). Freight delivery arrives immediately at your loading dock.

📦 2. Processing & Refurbishing (Unpacking Bay)

Visit the Unpacking Bay and click Tear Wrap ✂️ on delivered pallets. New stock is uninspected, so work each card through the small readiness flow:

  • Inspect: reveal grade, demand, and the condition note.
  • Test: discover hidden faults before a customer does.
  • Repair: optional; pay the item-specific quote to remove a fault and recover some value.

🏷️ 3. Pricing Strategy & Storefront

Display items on your Storefront Shelves and pick your pricing tags:

  • Fair / -15%: demand-sensitive shoppers can buy instantly from the shelf.
  • +30%: a customer archetype may carry it to the POS Register with a negotiation offer.
  • Online: install the $140 Online Marketplace + Shipping upgrade, then list inspected and tested items from the backroom or shelves. Remote orders pay an 8% marketplace fee.

🛎️ 4. Store Hours & Register Management

Click 🚪 Open Doors to start your 30-second shift. When a red badge appears on POS Register, a customer is waiting at the counter! You can accept their card, take cash and dispense change, or refuse them.

🚨 5. Shoplifters & Daily Rent

Watch out! Thieves will occasionally try to pocket items during business hours. Click Tackle Thief within 4 seconds to retrieve your stock and collect a $25 bounty. Buy security cameras on your PC to eliminate theft permanently. Save after installing upgrades so your new depot layout comes back with the rest of your stock.

Warning: At the end of each shift, make sure the store is closed, then click 🌙 End Day to pay your $35 commercial landlord rent. Falling into bankruptcy resets your store! Use Save Game on the Office PC after major purchases.
