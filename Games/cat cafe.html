❓ How to Play Guide

Welcome to The Cozy Cat & Coffee Nook! Your goal is to build the ultimate cozy sanctuary:

☕ 1. Brew & Bake: Prepare coffee batches and bake delicious pastries to earn Coins and Hearts.
🐱 2. Adopt & Pet Cats: Adopt cats from the Sanctuary! Each cat grants passive bonuses. Click cats directly on the canvas floor to pet them for hearts!
🔦 3. Laser Pointer: Toggle Laser Toy Mode on the floor to lead cats around and generate bonus purrs!
⭐ 4. Serve Patrons: Click sitting patrons when order bubbles appear above their heads before their patience bar runs out!
💰 5. Lucky Coin Bags: Keep an eye out for lucky coin bags dropped on the floor by cats like Clover!
