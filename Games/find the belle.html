📖 Detective's Field Guide

👋 Welcome to Belle's tiny cozy hiding game!
🎯 Cozy Hunt Objective: Belle hides in 1 of 32 spots across 4 rooms. Search the house and find her favorite little secret spot.
⚡ Zoomies Mode Explained: Belle gets the midnight crazies! You get 30 seconds on a live countdown clock to locate her.
• Finding her with ≥ 15s left awards +3 🏆 Trophies!
• Finding her with under 15s awards +2 🏆 Trophies.
• If time runs out, Belle escapes into another room!
💖 Pet & Purr Engine: Whenever you uncover Belle, rub her cheeks on screen! Fill the Purr-o-Meter to 100% to claim a bonus 🏆.
✨ Costumes with Real Perks: Spend trophies in the Closet to equip outfits that grant powers—such as adding extra Zoomies time (Warm Beanie), making purrs 2x faster (Fancy Bow), or adding extra treats (Chef Toque).
🧦 Swiped Hoard & Detective Tools: Empty spots drop random stolen treasures. Shake treats (🐟) to hear directional meows, or use Radar (🔴) and Light (🔦) to track her down.
💾 Multiple Save Profiles: Click the 💾 Slot button anytime to maintain up to 3 separate profiles or reset progress independently.
