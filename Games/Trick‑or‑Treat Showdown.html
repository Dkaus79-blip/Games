🎃 How to Play 🎃

• Place your 5 Halloween ships on your board:

  🎃 Pumpkin Mansion (5)

  👻 Ghost Manor (4)

  🕸️ Spider Shack (3)

  🧙 Witch Cottage (3)

  🍬 Candy Shack (2)

• Tap a ship, then tap a tile to preview placement.

• Tap Rotate to switch horizontal/vertical.

• Yellow preview = valid, red preview = invalid.

• During the game, click the AI board to attack.

• Red = hit (emoji appears), Blue = miss.

• Sink all 5 ships to win!
