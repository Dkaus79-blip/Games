🎯 The Objective & Quotas

Each Ante consists of 3 Quotas: Small Blind, Big Blind, and Boss Blind. You have 5 tickets per Blind to accumulate enough round score to meet the quota. Reach Ante 8 to beat the Syndicate!

🎟️ The 5 Ticket Games & Pay Tables

  • Cyber Triad ($10): Uncover 3 identical matching glyphs to win that symbol's listed prize (🍒 $12, 🍀 $20, ⭐ $35, 🌙 $60, 💎 $100, 👑 $250). Multiple sets stack!
  • Vault 99 ($25): Scratch the 6 key vaults. Match either Key Code to win the printed cash amount ($35 to $160).
  • Hyper 7 ($45): Reveal glowing 7s. 1 seven = $25 (push), 2 sevens = $75, 3 sevens = $180, 4 sevens = $500!
  • Neon 21 ($60): Scratch 3 hands to beat Dealer score. 1 Hand = $90, 2 Hands = $180, 3 Hands = $360.
  • Matrix Code ($85): Decrypt chips for N-E-O-N letters. 2 hits = $70, 3 hits = $150, 4 hits = $320, 5+ hits = $750.
  • Wager: Standard uses the listed price. High Stakes costs $5 more and increases winning payouts by 40%. The catalog shows each ticket's base win chance and prize profile.
